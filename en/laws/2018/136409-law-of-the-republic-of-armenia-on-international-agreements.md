# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENTS

> Pursuant to the Constitution of the Republic of Armenia, this Law shall enter into force on the day the newly-elected President of the Republic of Armenia assumes his or her office.
