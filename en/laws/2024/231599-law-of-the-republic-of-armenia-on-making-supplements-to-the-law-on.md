# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON MAKING SUPPLEMENTS TO THE LAW “ON BANK SECRECY”

**OFFICIAL TRANSLATION**

LAW

**OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA**

Adopted on 5 December 2024

**ON MAKING SUPPLEMENTS TO THE LAW “ON BANK SECRECY”**

## Article 1.

## Article 2. The Law shall be supplemented with Article 13.6 which shall read as follows:

<table>
<tr><td>“Article 13.6.</td><td>Provision of bank secrecy to the Oversight and Audit Service</td></tr>
</table>

1. In the cases prescribed by the Electoral Code of the Republic of Armenia, the provision of information constituting bank secrecy to the Oversight and Audit Service shall not be considered as disclosure of bank secrecy.

2. Provision of information constituting bank secrecy prescribed by the Constitutional Law of the Republic of Armenia “On referendum” and the Law “On local referendum” to the Oversight and Audit Service shall not be considered as disclosure of bank secrecy.

## Article 3. This Law shall enter into force on 1 January 2025.
