# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON MAKING A SUPPLEMENT TO THE LAW &quot;ON THE CENTRAL BANK OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA&quot;

OFFICIAL TRANSLATION?xml:namespace prefix = "o" ns = "urn:schemas-microsoft-com:office:office" /

**LAW**

**OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA**

Adopted on 3 July 2025

**ON MAKING A SUPPLEMENT TO THE LAW "ON THE CENTRAL BANK OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA"**

## Article 1. In point "g" of part 4 of Article 18 of Law HO-69 of 30 June 1996 "On the Central Bank of the Republic of Armenia", the words ", as well as the Law "On combating money laundering and financing of terrorism"" shall be added after the words "this Law".

## Article 2. This Law shall enter into force on the tenth day following the date of its official promulgation.
