# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON MAKING SUPPLEMENTS TO THE LAW “ON BANKS AND BANKING”

**OFFICIAL TRANSLATION**

**LAW**

**OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA**

Adopted on 29 May 2025

**ON MAKING SUPPLEMENTS TO THE LAW “ON BANKS AND BANKING”**

## Article 1. Part 2 of Article 2 of Law HO-68 of 30 June 1996 “On banks and banking” (hereinafter referred to as “the Law”) shall be supplemented with the words “crypto-asset service providers,” after the words “investment companies,”.

## Article 2. Point “p” of part 1 of Article 21.6 of the Law shall be supplemented with the words “, as well as electronic money tokens” after the words “and other securities of the bank”.

## Article 3. Part 1 of Article 34 of the Law shall be supplemented with point 19 which shall read as follows:

“(19) provide crypto-asset services provided for by the Law of the Republic of Armenia “On crypto-assets.”.

## Article 4. Part 6 of Article 36 of the Law shall be supplemented with the words “crypto-asset service providers,” after the words “investment companies,”.

## Article 5. Part 2 of Article 43 of the Law shall be supplemented with point “e” which shall read as follows:

“(e) in case of placement of electronic money tokens issued by the bank, information in the volume and in the manner prescribed by the Law of the Republic of Armenia “On crypto-assets” and the regulatory legal acts adopted on the basis thereof.”.

## Article 6. Article 61 of the Law shall be supplemented with part 4 which shall read as follows:

“4. The Central Bank shall apply measures of liability as prescribed by the Law of the Republic of Armenia “On crypto-assets” for violations of the requirements of legal acts regulating the market in crypto-assets provided for by Article 2 of the Law of the Republic of Armenia “On crypto-assets”, other regulatory legal acts adopted on the basis thereof, including the internal legal acts on providing crypto-asset services.”.

## Article 7. Article 72 of the Law shall be supplemented with the words “crypto-asset service providers,” after the words “investment companies,”.

## Article 8. This Law shall enter into force on the tenth day following the day of its official promulgation.
