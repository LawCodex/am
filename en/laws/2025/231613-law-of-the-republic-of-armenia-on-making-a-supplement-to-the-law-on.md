# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON MAKING A SUPPLEMENT TO THE LAW “ON INSURANCE AND INSURANCE ACTIVITIES”

**OFFICIAL TRANSLATION**

**LAW**

**OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA**

Adopted on 17 December 2025

**ON MAKING A SUPPLEMENT TO THE LAW “ON INSURANCE AND INSURANCE ACTIVITIES”**

## Article 1.

“4.1. This Law shall not extend to the relations provided for by the Law “On universal health insurance”.”

## Article 2. This Law shall enter into force on the day following its official promulgation.
