# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON MAKING AMENDMENTS AND A SUPPLEMENT TO THE LAW “ON COMPULSORY INSURANCE AGAINST LIABILITY ARISING FROM THE USE OF MOTOR VEHICLES”

**OFFICIAL TRANSLATION**

LAW

**OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA**

Adopted on 16 April 2026

**ON MAKING AMENDMENTS AND A SUPPLEMENT TO THE LAW “ON COMPULSORY INSURANCE AGAINST LIABILITY ARISING FROM THE USE OF MOTOR VEHICLES”**

Article 1. In part 5 of Article 29 of Law HO-63-N of 18 May 2010 “On compulsory insurance against liability arising from the use of motor vehicles” (hereinafter referred to as “the Law”), the words “in a media with a circulation of at least 1 000 copies,” shall be deleted, and the words “, on the official website for public notifications of the Republic of Armenia at http://www.azdarar.am” shall be added after the words “on the website thereof”.

Article 2. In the second sentence of part 2 of Article 30 of the Law, the words “in press,” shall be replaced with the words “on the official website for public notifications of the Republic of Armenia at http://www.azdarar.am, in”, and in the same sentence, the words “through mass media and/or” shall be deleted after the words “information bulletins”.

## Article 3. In part 3 of Article 34 of the Law, the words "in a nation-wide media with a circulation of at least 1 000 copies" shall be replaced with the words “on the official website for public notifications of the Republic of Armenia at http://www.azdarar.am”.

## Article 4. This Law shall enter into force on the tenth day following the day of its official promulgation.
