# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON MAKING SUPPLEMENTS TO THE LAW “ON INSURANCE AND INSURANCE ACTIVITIES”

**OFFICIAL TRANSLATION**

**LAW**

**OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA**

Adopted on 16 April 2026

**ON MAKING SUPPLEMENTS TO THE LAW “ON INSURANCE AND INSURANCE ACTIVITIES”**

Article 1. Part 4 of Article 111 of Law HO-177-N of 9 April 2007 “On insurance and insurance activities” (hereinafter referred to as “the Law”) shall be supplemented with the words “, in a closed envelope signed — on the part of the envelope where it is closed — by the head of the executive body or the person substituting for the head of the executive body” after the words “based on a court decision”.

## Article 2. Part 2 of Article 112 of the Law shall be supplemented with the words “, or electronically” after the words “in a closed envelope signed — on the part of the envelope where it is closed — by the head of the executive body or the person substituting for the head of the executive body”.

## Article 3. Part 2 of Article 113 of the Law shall be supplemented with the words “, or electronically” after the words “in a closed envelope signed — on the part of the envelope where it is closed —by the head of the executive body or the person substituting for the head of the executive body”.

## Article 4. This Law shall enter into force on the tenth day following the day of its official promulgation.
